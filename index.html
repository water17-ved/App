❓ Help
❓ Help & Welcome Tour ▼

New here, or just want a refresher on how the app is laid out?

📖 Manual — How This Tracker Works

A quick walkthrough of every section, so nothing feels like a mystery button.

🧭 Navigation & Interface
👑 HUD ▼
  • Level / XP bar Your overall level, driven by checklist completion, tests logged, and streak.
  • Streak Consecutive days you've marked yourself present. Missing a day resets it.
  • Mark Present Tap once per day to log attendance and keep your streak alive.
  • Battle Tag A name you set so you can appear on the shared Leaderboard.
📊 Subject Completion Cards ▼

The cards under the HUD show each subject's checklist % and chapters completed at a glance.

🧭 Moving the Navigation Bar ▼

The bottom navigation bar starts locked in place so it can't be moved by accident. To reposition it, go to Settings → 🎨 Customization → 🧭 Navigation Bar Position and unlock it — then grab the small dotted strip (⠿⠿) along its top edge and drag it anywhere on screen. Lock it again once you've found a good spot; its position is remembered next time you open the app.

📲 Home Screen Shortcuts ▼

Once installed (Add to Home Screen / APK), long-press the app icon for quick actions: Mark Present, Daily Qs, Battle Log, and Data & Backup — each jumps straight to that screen without opening the app fully first.

🚀 Splash Screen ▼

A brief themed loading screen shows on launch while your saved data loads, instead of a blank flash of white/black.

🎬 Stories Tab ▼

A themed recap card for each subject (and Advanced), plus your all-time question count pulled from the Daily Qs log. The same count appears as a small badge on each subject's nav button.

📚 Study Tracking
📝 Daily Qs Tab ▼

Log your day-to-day practice: pick a subject, difficulty level, how many questions you attempted, and which exam it was for (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or a custom "Other" exam). Every entry feeds the subject totals shown on the Story panel and tab badges, and counts toward your daily streak.

⏱ Time Tracking (Daily Qs) ▼

The Daily Qs form has an optional "Time Taken (min)" field. Once filled alongside question count, the day's summary and Weekly Report both show your average pace (seconds per question) — useful for gauging exam-day speed, not just accuracy.

📚 Study Hours ▼

Below the Daily Qs table, a small box to log today's total self-study hours per subject (Physics/Chemistry/Maths). Saving again today overwrites today's entry rather than duplicating it. The Weekly Report shows your average daily self-study hours (per subject and total) across the last 7 days.

⚛ 🧪 ➗ Physics / Chemistry / Maths Tabs ▼

Each chapter has a checklist (Reference Book, NCERT Theory, PYQs by year range, Notes, Lecture). Ticking items updates that chapter's %, the subject summary, and your overall XP.

🌌 JEE Advanced Tab ▼

A separate, tougher checklist layer for chapters you're pushing to Advanced level, with its own counters and its own Weak Spot Radar (Anomaly Scan) — kept independent from the Mains-only Danger Zone.

🔁 Revision Counter (per chapter) ▼

Every Physics/Chemistry/Maths chapter card has a small "Revisions" row with − and + buttons and a count in between. Tap + each time you go back and revise that chapter — a manual tally separate from the checklist and from Revision Due, just to track how many passes you've made over a topic.

🔁 Revision Due ▼

In the Booyah Badges tab, alongside the Weak Spot Radar. Once a chapter's checklist first hits 90%+, its completion date is remembered. If 20+ days pass with no chapter test logged for it since, it shows up here — a nudge to revisit chapters you've marked done rather than only touching them once.

📅 Study Reminders (70% chapters) ▼

The moment a chapter's checklist first crosses 70%, its date is remembered. Day 2 and day 5 nudge you to revise your notes; day 8+ nudges you to take a test on it. Shows in the Booyah Badges tab below Revision Due, and if Streak Reminders are on, sends one evening notification a day when something's due.

🎯 Battle Log & Tests
🎯 Battle Log Tab ▼
  • Danger Zone The most critical Mains chapters right now — badly scoring or fully checklist-complete but never tested. Only these entries get a "try a 50-question test" suggestion.
  • Chapter Skirmish Log Log per-chapter test results to track topic-level performance.
  • Final Zone Log Log full-length mock test results.
  • Mock Score Trend A line chart of your mock % over time — log at least 2 dated mocks to see it.
🏷 Mistake Type Tagging ▼

The Chapter Skirmish Log has optional Mistake Type chips (Silly Mistake / Concept Gap / Time Pressure / Guessed Wrong / None) — tap as many as apply to one test, not just one. The Weak Spot Radar shows your most common mistake type per weak chapter — so you know not just what's weak, but why.

📊 Mock Analysis (Final Zone) ▼

The Final Zone mock form is laid out as one row per subject — Physics / Chemistry / Maths, each with just Attempted (out of 25) and Correct. Total score and negative marks are calculated automatically (+4 per correct, −1 per wrong MCQ). There's also an optional "Topics Covered" field shown in the table and printable report.

The "Mock Analysis" panel below the trend chart shows all-time average section scores, plus — for whichever mock you're viewing — a segmented bar per subject: green (correct), red (wrong), and grey (unattempted), all out of 25. Tap any row in the Final Zone Log table to switch the analysis to that specific mock.

🧩 Custom Test Log ▼

In the Battle Log tab, below the Mock Score Trend chart. You name the category yourself (PYQ Paper, Speed Drill, whatever) — for any test that doesn't fit "chapter test" or "full mock." Add as many sections as you like with + Add Section, each with its own name, Qs, Score and Max Score — total score/% are added up automatically. Tap "N sections ▾" in the table to see the per-section breakdown.

✎ Editable Entries ▼

Every test log — Chapter Skirmish, Final Zone, Advanced (both), Daily Qs, and Custom — has an edit (✎) button next to delete on each row. It fills the form with that entry's values and the Add button switches to "✓ Update Entry"; a "Cancel Edit" link appears if you want to back out without saving.

↩ Undo ▼

Deleting a chapter test, mock test, or daily question shows an "Undo" toast at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds — tap it to instantly restore what you just removed.

📊 Analysis & Progress
📊 Chapter Analysis Tab ▼

Pick Physics, Chemistry, or Maths at the top for a full chapter-by-chapter breakdown: priority arrow, difficulty tag, estimated PYQ pool, weightage %, easy/medium/hard split, and a one-line strategic action plan per chapter, sorted highest-priority first. A weightage bar chart sits above the chapter cards, and below them is a year-wise question distribution chart with a dropdown to isolate a single chapter's trend.

⬆️⬇️ Chapter Priority Emojis ▼

Each chapter shows a small arrow next to its name: ⬆️ = high-yield, no arrow = moderate, ⬇️ = lower-yield. Calculated from the official JEE Mains & Advanced chapter-wise question distribution (2015–2026) — each chapter's average Qs/shift in Mains and total Qs in Advanced.

📝🎓 Mains / Advanced Chips ▼

Open a chapter card in Chapter Analysis and you'll see two small chips — 📝 Mains: X–Y Qs/shift and 🎓 Advanced: X–Y Qs — the exact real range from the official distribution, not an estimate. A chapter with no Advanced chip simply doesn't appear there.

📅 Per-Chapter Year View ▼

On any chapter card in the Mathematics tab where year-wise data exists, a small 📅 button sits next to the chapter title. Tap it to expand a mini bar chart right there showing how many questions that chapter contributed each year from 2019 to 2026 — no need to leave the checklist.

🗺 Roadmap (Progress Tab) ▼

A prerequisite map — for each chapter, what to finish first and what it unlocks next, grouped into three study phases (Foundation → Bridge → High-Yield). It's a guide, not a gate — nothing in the checklist gets locked. Pick a subject to see its full dependency chain.

⚖️ Subject Balance Nudge ▼

Appears automatically if one subject is far ahead of another (25%+ gap in checklist completion) — a nudge to stop over-preparing one subject while neglecting another, a common cause of lopsided scores.

🖨 Reports & Printing
🖨 Weekly Report ▼

Opens right inside the app — a printable summary of the last 7 days: checklist progress, questions practiced (per subject + total), tests logged, streak, and present days. Use the panel's Print button (or Download to save the .html file first) to share it with a parent or mentor.

🖨 Printable Mock Analysis (All-Time) ▼

A button below the Mock Analysis panel opens a full printable report covering every Final Zone mock ever logged — average score, best/weakest mock, trend, section-wise averages, negative-marking analysis, and a dedicated "Analysis of Every Test" section giving each mock its own breakdown. Opens in the same in-app preview panel as the Weekly Report.

🖨 Print This Mock ▼

Next to the all-time report button — prints only whichever single mock you're currently viewing in the Mock Analysis panel. Tap a row in the Final Zone Log table to pick one first. Useful when you want one test's breakdown on paper without the full history.

🖨 All-Time Daily Qs Report ▼

A button below the Daily Qs table opens a printable report covering every question ever logged — totals per subject, total time logged, average pace, and the full history grouped day-by-day: each date gets its own heading, subtotal, and mini-table.

💾 Data & Backup
💾 Auto-Save ▼

Every checklist tick, test entry, and daily log is saved automatically to this device's local storage the instant you make it — nothing to remember, no sync step, and it works fully offline.

⬇ Download & ⬆ Restore Backup ▼

Download Backup exports everything (checklists, tests, streak, daily questions) as a .json file — useful if you're moving to a new phone or want an extra copy. Restore Backup loads a previously downloaded .json, replacing current data on this device.

🗑 Reset All Data ▼

Wipes everything on this device — checklists, tests, streak, daily log. Opens a confirmation popup where you must type RESET before it will proceed, since it can't be undone. Download a backup first if you might want the data later.

🏆 Achievements & Social
🏆 Booyah Badges Tab ▼

Achievements you unlock through consistent progress, plus the Weak Spot Radar — chapters dragging your average down across both checklist and test performance.

🌐 Leaderboard Tab ▼

Sign in with Google via the account bar at the top of the app to join the live, online leaderboard — everyone logged in is ranked by League (weekly XP tier) first, plus separate tables for average mock score and subject-wise syllabus completion. Your name, league, score summary, mock test log, and daily question log are shared while "Show me on leaderboard" is on; checklists and the coaching log always stay local on your device.

⚙️ Settings Reference
🎨 Accent Theme ▼

Pick from 5 palettes, tailored to whichever interface you're using — your choice is remembered separately per interface. Four of the five only change the gold/highlight accent; Physics/Chemistry/Maths keep their fixed colors so subject color-coding still makes sense. Minimal Mono is the same in every interface: a true black-and-white theme — pitch black background, white text, grayscale everywhere, no glow or animation.

🔤 Font Pack ▼

Pick between three body text styles: Gaming (bold Space Mono), Roboto (clean default), or Modern (Inter — polished and highly readable). Headers/HUD/titles always stay on Orbitron and buttons/tabs on Rajdhani regardless of which you pick; only the general body text changes.

🔔 Streak Reminders ▼

Asks for notification permission, then checks at your chosen reminder time (default 8:00 PM) whether you've logged anything today. If not, it fires a local reminder. Since this app has no server, this only works while the app/browser is open in the background — not a guaranteed push notification like WhatsApp.

🔊 Sound Effects & 📳 Haptics ▼

Sound Effects: Short chimes for ticking a checkbox, completing a chapter, logging a test or daily questions, marking yourself present, leveling up, and deleting entries. Generated on-device (no audio files), so they work fully offline.

Haptics: Small vibrations on ticks, level-ups, streaks, and deletes — toggle independently from sound. Only works on devices that support the Vibration API (mainly Android); shows "Unsupported" on iOS Safari and most desktop browsers.

🚀 Welcome Tour ▼

First time opening the app, you'll get a quick 5-step walkthrough of the HUD, Practice tabs, Progress tab, and customization options. You can skip it any time — and replay it from Settings → Help → "Replay Welcome Tour".
